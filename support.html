How do I add my channels?

Open Sources, paste your playlist link (or Xtream server, username and password) and press Open & save.

I see "The server could not be reached"

Check the link in a browser first. Some providers publish a different address for phones than for TVs. Large lists (tens of thousands of channels) can take a minute to load.

Cancel or manage my subscription

iPhone/iPad: Settings ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions. Android: Google Play ▸ Payments & subscriptions. Huawei: AppGallery ▸ Me ▸ Subscriptions.

Restore purchases

Open the subscription screen or Settings ▸ About and tap Restore.
