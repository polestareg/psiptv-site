1. Who we are

PS IPTV is an app for playing the IPTV playlists that you add yourself. It is developed by Waleed Lotfy and published as PS-Tech. Contact: support@marblefind.com

2. What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect data about you and the app sends nothing to our servers. There is no account or sign-in, and no analytics, advertising or tracking.

3. What is stored on your device

The playlist links and streaming-server logins you add, your favourites, bouquets and settings, and playlist files you choose to save. They stay on your device only, can be encrypted with a PIN, and are removed when you uninstall the app.

4. Network connections

The app connects only to what you choose: the playlist server you added; the public channel lists of the iptv-org project when you open the "Free" tab; and OpenSubtitles when you use subtitle search with your own key (it receives the title you search for and your key, under its own privacy policy).

5. Subscription and payment

The free trial and the yearly subscription are handled by the Apple App Store, Google Play or Huawei AppGallery. The store alone processes payment; we never see your card details. We only receive the subscription status (active or not).

6. Content

PS IPTV does not provide or host any channels or movies. You are responsible for the sources you add and for having the right to watch them.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data about them.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will show the date above. Continued use means you accept the updated version.
